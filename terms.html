Submitting the registration form is an application to join. Membership begins when your payment is received and we confirm it by email. The board may decline an application, and if we do, any payment is refunded in full.

Membership is annual and the fee is shown on the Become a Member page at the time you join. Fees are in Australian dollars. Your membership runs for twelve months from the date we confirm it.

We will contact you before your membership is due to expire. You are not locked into an ongoing commitment beyond the twelve months you have paid for.

You can cancel at any time by emailing info@sctechia.com.au. Because membership is an annual arrangement, fees already paid are generally not refunded, though the board will consider genuine hardship or an error on our part. Nothing here limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
