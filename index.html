Who we are

We are the gateway to the region's digital future.

The Sunshine Coast Tech Alliance is the peak industry body representing and connecting the Sunshine Coast digital economy. We bring together technology businesses, digital leaders, innovators, educators, government, investors and industry to drive growth, capability and opportunity across the region.

As an independent, industry-led organisation, we champion the people, businesses and technologies shaping the future of the Sunshine Coast and ensure our region is recognised as a leading destination for innovation, digital transformation and technology-enabled growth.

Vision

A thriving Sunshine Coast digital economy recognised nationally for innovation, capability and collaboration.

Mission

Connecting, growing and representing the Sunshine Coast digital economy.

What we represent

Eight focus areas across the Sunshine Coast digital economy.

We represent the entire digital ecosystem - from infrastructure and cybersecurity through to creative technology and workforce development.

Digital Infrastructure

Broadband, subsea cables, data centres, cloud infrastructure, connectivity, smart cities. The region has unique assets including international connectivity and data infrastructure that very few regional locations possess.

Artificial Intelligence & Automation

Generative AI, machine learning, automation, robotics, productivity technology. AI is no longer a niche sector. It is becoming the technology layer across every industry.

Cyber Security & Digital Trust

Cybersecurity, privacy, resilience, identity management, governance. Every industry now has cyber requirements. This will only accelerate toward Brisbane 2032.

Digital Transformation

Enterprise systems, software, SaaS, ERP, CRM, cloud migration, process automation. Most Sunshine Coast businesses are not tech companies, but they all rely on the tools of one.

Emerging Technologies

Advanced manufacturing technology, robotics, digital twins, spatial computing, quantum, defence technologies, future industries. Keeps SCTechIA future-focused and allows the organisation to evolve.

Digital Media & Creative Technologies

Gaming, screen production, digital media, immersive technologies, content creation, marketing technology. This sector is growing rapidly and is often overlooked by traditional technology groups - by collaborating with the Screen Collective, we support this sub-ecosystem.

Talent, Skills & Future Workforce

Education, pathways, attraction, retention, migration, upskilling, leadership development. Industry forums identify workforce as the number one challenge - through collaborations with Study Sunshine Coast and the Sunshine Coast Regional Jobs Committee, the Tech Alliance supports and promotes tech pathways.

Tech Enablement

Hands-on help adopting and using technology, not just hearing about it - training, implementation support and practical programs like our AI workshops that build real capability inside Sunshine Coast businesses.

What makes us different

We are a community creating real outcomes.

We are more than a networking organisation. We are a community of industry leaders, practitioners and advocates working together to create real outcomes for the region.

Unlike organisations focused on a single sector, technology or profession, we connect the entire digital economy ecosystem. We combine industry insight, thought leadership, workforce development, business growth and regional advocacy to create opportunities that would not exist in isolation.

Our focus is not simply on technology itself, but on the people, businesses and partnerships that technology enables. We measure success by the connections made, collaborations formed, talent developed, businesses strengthened and opportunities created for our region.
